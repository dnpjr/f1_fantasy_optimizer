# Modelling evidence

These generated reports are intentionally retained: they document the progression from exploratory Sprint multipliers and linear fits to partial pooling and the selected production EV model. They are research evidence, not independent predictive-accuracy claims.

- `sprint_multiplier/`, `sprint_linear_regression/`, `2026_sprint_bonus/`: exploratory definitions, fits and sensitivity checks.
- `2026_personalised_sprint_adjustments/`, `2026_sprint_partial_pooling/`, `2026_sprint_final_candidate/`: model selection and frozen round-11 comparisons. Some CSVs are explicit inputs to subsequent reproducibility checks.
- `2026_sprint_shadow_comparison/`: fixed historical production/shadow experiment.
- `sprint_recalibration_2026_round_12/`: accepted v2 update, parameter comparisons, source coverage and promotion record.
- `sprint_updates/`: future versioned bundles generated by the maintenance command. Keep the complete reviewed/activated bundle, including frozen inputs and validation receipt.

`reports/sprint_recalibration_candidate/` is a superseded unversioned scratch output and remains ignored locally. Do not activate it. The runtime reads calibration artifacts under `data/generated/`; it does not execute research builders or refit at startup.

Follow [Sprint maintenance](../docs/SPRINT_MODEL_MAINTENANCE.md) for future production changes. Historical reports describe the data/model at their creation date, not necessarily the current live market or active calibration.
